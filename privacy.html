ParentPlay Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

About ParentPlay

ParentPlay is designed to help parents and carers find suitable activities, places and ideas for their children. We aim to use only the information needed to provide these features.

Child profiles

You can create child profiles containing information such as a name or nickname, age, avatar and interests. These profiles are stored locally on your device and are used to personalise activity suggestions and age filtering.

ParentPlay does not send your child profile information with your location when requesting weather or nearby place information.

Location

With your permission, ParentPlay uses your device location to provide local weather information and help find relevant activities and places nearby.

Location access is optional. You can deny or change location permission through your device settings. Some weather and nearby-place features may not work without location access.

When you use location-based features, location coordinates or location-based search information may be shared with the relevant third-party service providers to return weather forecasts and nearby places.

Online services

To provide weather, nearby-place and venue information, ParentPlay communicates with third-party online services. Depending on the feature being used, your device's location coordinates, location-based search terms and technical request information may be transmitted to these services.

ParentPlay currently uses:

  • Open-Meteo for local weather information — Terms and privacy information.
  • Google Places for venue searches and photographs — Google Privacy Policy.
  • OpenStreetMap-based services, including Nominatim and Overpass, for geographical and place information — OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.

These third-party providers may process technical information associated with requests, such as IP addresses, device or request information, in accordance with their own privacy policies and service terms. Some providers may process information outside the United Kingdom.

ParentPlay does not include children's names, avatars or interests from locally saved child profiles in these requests.

Favourites and preferences

Favourites and certain app preferences are stored locally on your device so ParentPlay can remember your choices.

External links

ParentPlay may allow you to open third-party websites, map services or search results. When you choose to open an external service, that service may process information under its own privacy policy.

Advertising and analytics

The current version of ParentPlay does not use advertising, advertising identifiers or analytics tracking. If this changes in a future version, this privacy policy will be updated as appropriate.

Data retention and deletion

ParentPlay stores child profiles, favourites and certain app preferences locally on your device.

You can manage this information through the app's Privacy & Data Controls section, which provides three options:

  • Delete child profile: Remove a selected child's profile without deleting other profiles or saved favourites.
  • Delete saved favourites: Remove saved places and activities without affecting child profiles.
  • Delete all information: Remove locally saved child profiles, favourites and app preferences.

Each deletion action requires confirmation.

You can also clear ParentPlay's app data through your device settings or uninstall the app.

Deleting locally stored information does not necessarily delete information previously processed or retained by third-party service providers. Their retention practices are governed by their respective privacy policies.

Security

We take reasonable steps to minimise the personal information used by ParentPlay and to limit access to information needed for app functionality. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

ParentPlay is intended for use by parents and carers. It is not designed for children to create accounts or independently provide personal information to ParentPlay.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy when ParentPlay features, services or legal requirements change. The latest version will show its updated date.

Contact us

For questions about privacy or how ParentPlay handles information, contact: support@parentplay.co.uk
