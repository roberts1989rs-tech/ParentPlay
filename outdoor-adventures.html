Easy outdoor adventure ideas

Try a nature colour hunt, a leaf-spotting walk, a picnic trail, a bug observation activity or a local wildlife walk.

Choose a suitable route

Consider the distance, terrain, toilets, parking and whether the route works for pushchairs or younger children.

Prepare for the weather

Pack layers, waterproofs, water and suitable footwear. Check local forecasts and avoid unsafe conditions.

Look after nature

Stick to permitted paths, leave plants and wildlife undisturbed, and take litter home.
