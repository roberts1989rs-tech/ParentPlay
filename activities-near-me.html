Finding things to do nearby

A useful local search starts with your area, children’s ages, the weather and how long you have. ParentPlay is designed to bring these considerations together in its app.

Try more than one type of activity

Look for local libraries, leisure centres, play areas, nature reserves, museums, community events and family-friendly attractions.

Search smartly

Try combinations such as “children’s activities near me”, “family days out near me” or the name of your town plus “kids activities”.

Check the details

Confirm the actual location, opening hours, prices, availability and suitability before travelling. This webpage does not automatically detect your location.
