Children’s Farms, Petting Farms & Animal Days Out

Explore ideas for children’s farms, petting farms and animal experiences in the UK, plus tips for planning a safe, enjoyable family visit.

← Browse all activity guides

What to look for in a children’s farm

Check for supervised animal encounters, accessible paths, handwashing facilities, toilets and age-appropriate play areas.

Different animal adventures

Consider farm parks, wildlife centres, aquariums, birdwatching walks and organised animal experiences.

Health and hygiene

Wash hands thoroughly with soap and water after touching animals and before eating. Follow the venue’s hygiene guidance, especially around young children.

Plan your visit

Animal activities may be seasonal or timed. Check the venue’s current schedule and booking arrangements before leaving.
