Easy & Safe Science Experiments for Kids at Home

Try easy science experiments for children at home using everyday materials. Explore colourful, hands-on activities with adult supervision.

← Browse all activity guides

Walking water rainbow

Put water in three clear cups, add a little food colouring to two cups, then bridge the cups with folded kitchen paper. Watch water move through the paper by capillary action. Protect surfaces from stains.

Floating and sinking

Collect safe household objects, predict which will float in a bowl of water, then test your ideas and discuss why some objects behave differently.

Make a paper helicopter

Cut and fold a paper helicopter shape, then drop it from a low, safe height. Change the blade length and compare how it falls.

Safety for little scientists

An adult should supervise. Use non-toxic household materials, avoid flames, strong chemicals, sealed pressure experiments and tasting experiment materials. Keep small objects away from children who may swallow them.
