Free Things to Do With Kids in the UK

Discover free family activities, from local libraries and nature walks to creative projects at home. Fun ideas that need little or no budget.

← Browse all activity guides

Free days out

Try local parks, public walking trails, community events and museums with free entry. Some venues charge for special exhibitions or parking.

What your library might offer

Many libraries run reading sessions, crafts or holiday activities. Check your local library’s calendar for current sessions.

No-cost activities at home

Create a treasure hunt, build a den, invent a family quiz or use recycled materials for an art challenge.

Keep costs predictable

Check travel, parking, food and booking costs before setting off. “Free entry” does not always mean a completely free day.
