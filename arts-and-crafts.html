Arts & Crafts for Kids: Easy Creative Ideas

Explore simple arts and crafts for kids, including nature collages, paper crafts and recycled-material projects for family creative time.

← Browse all activity guides

Nature collage

Gather fallen leaves and twigs where collecting is permitted. Arrange them on paper and use child-safe glue to make a seasonal picture.

Cardboard town

Save clean packaging boxes and turn them into shops, houses and roads with paper, crayons and tape.

Paper puppet theatre

Make characters from paper and craft sticks, then put on a family puppet show with a story everyone helps write.

Make it age appropriate

Choose washable, non-toxic materials. Adults should handle sharp tools and supervise small parts and adhesives.
