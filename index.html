About ParentPlay

ParentPlay is a mobile app in the making for parents and carers. It’s being designed to make finding something fun to do together feel simpler, with activity ideas informed by local weather, nearby places, children’s ages and their interests.

Because the best days don’t need a big plan — just an idea that feels right for your family.
