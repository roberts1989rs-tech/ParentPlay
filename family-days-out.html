Choose a day out that suits your family

Start with the ages of the children, how far you want to travel, your budget and the weather. A short local outing can be just as memorable as a full day trip.

Ideas for a family day out

Try a hands-on museum, a woodland trail with a scavenger hunt, a farm visit, a swimming session or a free local park with a picnic.

Before you set off

Check opening hours, accessibility, booking requirements, parking and any age or height restrictions on the venue’s own website.

Make the most of the journey

Let children choose a theme for the day, pack snacks and a change of clothes, and keep a rainy-day backup in mind.
