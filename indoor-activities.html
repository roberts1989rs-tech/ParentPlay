Indoor Activities for Kids & Rainy Days

Find indoor activities for children, from museums and creative play to easy at-home projects. Make rainy days more fun with ParentPlay.

← Browse all activity guides

Indoor days out

Explore museums, libraries, leisure centres, soft-play venues, climbing centres and community workshops appropriate for your children’s ages.

Creative time at home

Build a blanket den, make paper puppets, create a family comic or turn recycled boxes into a miniature town.

Burn off energy indoors

Try a living-room obstacle course with safe, soft obstacles, a dance challenge or a gentle family stretching session.

Keep the day manageable

Mix active and quiet activities, allow for breaks, and check whether a venue requires advance booking.
