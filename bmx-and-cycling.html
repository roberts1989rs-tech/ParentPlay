BMX Tracks & Family Cycling Trails

Looking for BMX tracks or family cycling trails? Find ideas for beginner-friendly cycling adventures and what to check before visiting.

← Browse all activity guides

Find a suitable place to ride

Look for designated BMX tracks, pump tracks, traffic-free cycle paths and family cycling routes. Search by your town or area for nearby facilities.

Match the route to the rider

For beginners, choose smooth, quiet routes. BMX and pump tracks can vary significantly in difficulty and may have rules or age restrictions.

Safety first

Wear a correctly fitted helmet and suitable protective equipment, check the bike before riding and supervise children appropriately.

Before you travel

Check whether the track is public, open, maintained and suitable for the child’s ability. Respect local rules and other riders.
